The Legacy

At Lumière Atelier, we do not merely create windows; we choreograph light. Our practice is a dialogue between the raw volatility of molten glass and the disciplined precision of architectural geometry.

Founded in the heart of Lyon, our studio preserves the ancestral secrets of the medieval masters while embracing the mathematical possibilities of the modern age. Every installation is a unique atmospheric intervention, transforming a void into a sanctuary of chromatic depth.

We seek the intersection of the divine and the structural, ensuring that every shard of glass is placed with intention, every lead line drawn with purpose.
