Bespoke museum-quality stained glass installations for architectural spaces of enduring beauty and meaning

Where Century-Old Craft Meets Modern Vision

Founded in Lyon in 1924, Lumière Atelier has spent a century perfecting the art of stained glass. We create architectural installations that don't just adorn spaces—they transform them, infusing light with color, texture, and profound emotional resonance.

Our pieces live in the spaces between function and poetry: in cathedral windows that tell stories in light, in corporate lobbies where glass becomes a canvas for corporate identity, in private residences where every pane whispers of luxury and discernment.

We work exclusively with master artisans who have spent decades mastering the delicate alchemy of glass, color, and light. Each commission is a collaboration—a dialogue between our clients' visions and our century of expertise.

Our Process

01

Consultation

We begin with deep listening—understanding your space, your vision, and the emotional resonance you seek. Every commission starts with a conversation.

02

Design

Our designers translate your vision into sketches and full-scale cartoons, studying light patterns, color theory, and architectural harmony.

03

Creation

Master artisans hand-cut each piece of glass, selecting from our archive of 5,000+ colors and textures, then assemble with copper foil or lead.

04

Installation

We handle every detail of installation, ensuring your piece arrives at its permanent home with the care and precision it deserves.
