Where Light Becomes Sacred Architecture

Founded in 1987 beneath the vaulted arches of a converted Lyon tannery, Lumière Atelier exists at the singular intersection of medieval craft and contemporary vision. Our atelier does not merely fabricate stained glass; it composes with light itself, transmuting raw silica and mineral pigments into architectural instruments of illumination.

Each commission begins as a dialogue between the architect's intent and the material's ancient memory. We work exclusively with hand-blown antique glass, Kiln-fired dichroic surfaces, and custom-parements ground from oxides we source from a single atelier in Murano. The result is glass that breathes differently at dawn than at dusk — living, breathing architecture that rewrites itself with the passage of the sun.

Our pieces reside in private chapels on Saint-Tropez, in the atrium of a museum in Doha, and in homes across three continents. Every pane carries the maker's hand. Every composition is singular. Nothing is repeated. Nothing is ordinary.

The Commission

From First Light to Final Revelation
I

Conversation

We begin with an extended dialogue — understanding the architecture, the quality of light, the emotional resonance you seek. A site visit is not optional; it is essential. Light must be witnessed, not imagined.

II

Cartoon

Our designers produce full-scale cartoons — precise drawings on primed canvas. You will see exactly how light will fall, how color will shift through the hours. Revisions continue until the vision is absolute.

III

Realisation

Each piece of glass is cut, bevelled, and assembled by hand — a process measured in weeks, not days. Copper foil or lead came, fired patina, hand-polished joints. No machine touches the final work.

IV

Installation

Our master installers travel to your site. Every panel is fitted, sealed, and calibrated on location. We do not depart until the light sings precisely as we composed it.
